## Blanco (with northern Gorda Ridge)
Papers:
1. Braunmiller & Nábělek (2008). Segmentation of the Blanco Transform Fault Zone from earthquake analysis: Complex tectonics of an oceanic transform fault. JGR Solid Earth 113(B7). 10.1029/2007JB005213 — 129 regional source parameters + 28 Harvard MTs; along-fault seismic slip rates; segment/step-over layout (Gorda Depression, Blanco Ridge, Cascadia Depression); NE bias in routine epicenters.
2. Kuna, Nábělek & Braunmiller (2019). Mode of slip and crust–mantle interaction at oceanic transform faults. Nature Geoscience 12(2), 138–142. 10.1038/s41561-018-0287-1 — 2012–13 OBS: crust fully coupled; mantle above 600 °C creeps episodically, triggering swarms; mantle creep precedes large events. Bears on our depth spread and columns.
3. Liu & Tan (2025). Evaluating the performance of machine-learning-based phase pickers when applied to ocean bottom seismic data: Blanco oceanic transform fault as a case study. GJI 242(3), ggaf256. 10.1093/gji/ggaf256 — EQT vs PickBlue vs OBSTransformer catalogs; PickBlue most events, best locations; mislocations change slip-mode interpretation; gaps in three extensional segments (aseismic slip); most events above 600 °C; deep clusters only under Blanco Ridge. Closest method analogue.
4. Journeau, Thomas, Abercrombie, Hirao, Liu & Kuna (2026). OBS data mining reveals seismic structure and dynamics of the oceanic Blanco Transform Fault, Northeast Pacific. JGR Solid Earth 131(6), e2025JB032982. 10.1029/2025JB032982 — ML catalog >30,000 events, one year, 54 OBS; depths depend on velocity model/association/location; ~2.4× more events at step-overs; mantle bursts under locked M6 patches on Blanco Ridge.
5. Lange, Ren & Grevemeyer (2026). Seismicity, repeating earthquakes, and tomographic imaging of the Blanco Transform Fault System, Northeast Pacific. JGR Solid Earth 131(5), e2025JB031584. 10.1029/2025JB031584 — 12,708 events; on simple strands within 2 km of the trace; bursts <5 min; repeaters (west, Gorda Depression, E Blanco Ridge); Vp/Vs >2 (serpentinization); 3-D model to test our depths.
Also: Ren, Lange & Grevemeyer (2023) JGR Solid Earth 128(3) e2022JB026045 10.1029/2022JB026045 — 84 OBS focal mechanisms; Cascadia Depression intra-transform spreading.
Questions:
1. Along-strike seismic coupling and its stability over 2012–2015 (published views are one year). Counts and (later) moment per segment per year vs Braunmiller & Nábělek slip rates; does step-over contrast (Journeau) and extensional gaps (Liu & Tan) persist? Limits: coverage changes by year → Mc per year/segment; ML pending; 4 yr ≪ M6 recurrence (10–20 yr) → rates and b-values more robust than moment.
2. Deep events/columns: mantle swarms or poor depth control? Well-recorded subset (OBS within ~1 focal depth, S–P), depth vs 600 °C isotherm and Journeau/Lange for the overlap year; relocate with Lange 3-D model. Limits: datum (water 2–4 km ≈ crustal thickness); poor geometry outside X9/Z5 years.
3. Swarms/bursts and migration linked to larger events (Kuna's precursory creep)? Swarm detection, migration fits, activity before M5+ (2012–15), productivity Blanco Ridge vs extensional segments. Limits: few M5+; repeaters need cross-correlation; low-M completeness only in OBS years.
4. Northern Gorda Ridge swarms 2012–2015? Cheap space–time scan; null result weak (poor coverage south of 43°N offshore).

## Gorda plate / deformation zone
Papers:
1. Chaytor, Goldfinger, Dziak & Fox (2004). Active deformation of the Gorda plate: Constraining deformation models with new geophysical data. Geology 32(4), 353. 10.1130/G20178.2 — Mapped active faults; NE-striking left-lateral faults rotating under N–S compression. Check our lineation strikes.
2. Rollins & Stein (2010). Coulomb stress interactions among M ≥ 5.9 earthquakes in the Gorda deformation zone and on the Mendocino Fault Zone, Cascadia subduction zone, and northern San Andreas Fault. JGR Solid Earth 115(B12). 10.1029/2009JB007117 — 20 M≥5.9 since 1976 on four fault orientations; triggering ≥0.6 bar within <9 months. Framework for 2010 M6.5 and 2014 M6.8 aftershocks.
3. Chen & McGuire (2016). Measuring earthquake source parameters in the Mendocino triple junction region using a dense OBS array: Implications for fault strength variations. EPSL 453, 276–287. 10.1016/j.epsl.2016.08.022 — Stress drops from 7D OBS; same stations; check on our magnitudes.
4. Guo, McGuire & Zhang (2021). Correlation of porosity variations and rheological transitions on the southern Cascadia megathrust. Nature Geoscience 14(5), 341–348. 10.1038/s41561-021-00740-1 — 3-D Vp/Vs and relocations separating slab crust/mantle/interface; model to re-check our depths.
5. McPherson, Patton, Hellweg, Smith, Dengler, Lomax & Dreger (2025). Large repeating Gorda intraplate earthquakes occurring along an inherited weak zone near the Mendocino Triple Junction. SRL 96(5), 3179–3188. 10.1785/0220250005 — 2022 Mw6.4 Ferndale re-ruptured the 1975 fault; inherited weak zones may be common. Do 2010–2015 lineations mark them?
Also: Gong & McGuire (2022) BSSA 112(5) 2440–2453 10.1785/0120210261 — interface clusters with long-duration S vs impulsive S deeper.
Questions:
1. How is deformation shared between plate-interior faults, slab and megathrust north of the MTJ? Classify vs Slab2/Guo interface; lineation strikes vs Chaytor and our 112 matched tensors. Limits: datum; offshore west of ~125.5°W only 7D + distant land; near-coast depth mode may reflect geometry.
2. Aftershocks of 2010 M6.5 and 2014 M6.8: productivity, decay, extent, mutual triggering. ETAS/Omori once ML final; vs Rollins & Stein. Limits: ML pending; poor early completeness; only two events; no 7D in early 2010.
3. Persistent inherited weak zones (McPherson 2025)? Planar small-event clusters on the 1975/2022 Ferndale, 1980 and 2005 ruptures. Spatial test, no magnitudes needed. Limits: needs relative relocation; 6 yr cannot show recurrence.
4. Along-dip b-value/source-property variation in the slab? After magnitudes; changes ≤0.1 unresolved.
Not used: Ren, Lange & Grevemeyer EGU 2025 abstract (Gorda intraplate shear zones, 10.5194/egusphere-egu25-17345) — watch for journal version; Liu, Liu & Tan EGU 2026; Gong 2021 WHOI thesis; Kummerfeldt 2025 ESSOAr. No match for "Wang/Goldfinger" Blanco, Hellweg 2010 Eureka, Nicholson Gorda.
