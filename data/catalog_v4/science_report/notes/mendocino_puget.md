## Mendocino triple junction
Papers:
1. McCrory, Blair, Waldhauser & Oppenheimer (2012). Juan de Fuca slab geometry and its relation to Wadati-Benioff zone seismicity. JGR Solid Earth 117(B9), B09306. 10.1029/2012JB009407 — 3-D slab model from DD relocations; slab seismicity peaks where the slab is warped beneath NW California. Also Puget.
2. Chen & McGuire (2016). Measuring earthquake source parameters in the Mendocino triple junction region using a dense OBS array: Implications for fault strength variations. EPSL 453, 276–287. 10.1016/j.epsl.2016.08.022 — Same 7D OBS; >1000 events relocated 2012–13; NW–SE faults in the Gorda plate; lower stress drops near the plate boundary.
3. Materna, Taira & Bürgmann (2018). Aseismic transform fault slip at the Mendocino Triple Junction from characteristically repeating earthquakes. GRL 45(2), 699–707. 10.1002/2017GL075899 — Repeaters 2008–2017: eastern MFZ creeps ~65% of long-term rate; slower creep on Pacific–wedge interface and a Gorda intraplate fault; possible acceleration after 2015 M5.7.
4. Yoon & Shelly (2024). Distinct yet adjacent earthquake sequences near the Mendocino Triple Junction: 20 December 2021 Mw 6.1 and 6.0 Petrolia, and 20 December 2022 Mw 6.4 Ferndale. The Seismic Record 4(1), 81–92. 10.1785/0320230053 — DL + template matching; >14,000 added M0–2 events; separates Gorda crust, mantle and interface. Post-2016 (structure, not overlap).
5. Chamarczuk, Chen & Johnson (2026). Fifteen years of observations suggest structural and ocean-wave control on seismicity at the Mendocino triple junction. SRL 97(4), 2388–2398. 10.1785/0220250447 — Seasonal wave loading ±3–4 kPa changes rates 10–40% with months of lag; caution that detection is also seasonal.
Alternates: Furlong & Schwartz 2004 Annu Rev EPS 32 403–433 10.1146/annurev.earth.32.101802.120252; Gong & McGuire 2018 EPSL 482 414–422 10.1016/j.epsl.2017.11.022 (2015 M5.7, 2010 M6.5 afterslip, locked ≥14 km); Gong & McGuire 2021 JGR 126 e2020JB019962 10.1029/2020JB019962 (subducted Gorda geometry); Waldhauser 2009 BSSA 99 2736–2748 10.1785/0120080294 (DD catalog); McPherson et al. 2025; Guo et al. 2025 SRL 96(3) 1504–1520 10.1785/0220240078 (other agent dated it 2024).
Questions:
1. Share of MTJ seismicity on the Gorda–NA interface vs Gorda crust/mantle, NA crust, MFZ? Project onto McCrory 2012 / Gong & McGuire 2021; by year and sub-region; vs Waldhauser DD. Limits: datum; offshore depths outside OBS years; no per-event uncertainty → share is a range.
2. Did aseismic slip on the MFZ and Gorda interior change 2010–2016 (after 2014 M6.8, 2015 M5.7)? Clusters → repeater candidates → waveform cross-correlation; vs Materna 2018. Limits: needs waveform correlation; ML pending; moment–slip scaling.
3. Aftershocks of Jan 2010 M6.5 vs Mar 2014 M6.8: productivity, decay. ETAS/Omori above per-period Mc once ML final. Limits: 2014 count mixes aftershocks with better OBS coverage; offshore 2014 event; land-only periods.
4. Seasonal modulation (Chamarczuk 2026)? Monthly rates above Mc vs wave height. Limits: 6 yr short; ML detection noise-dependent (seasonal, esp. OBS); needs magnitudes.

## Puget Lowland
Papers:
1. Brocher, Parsons, Blakely, Christensen, Fisher, Wells & SHIPS Working Group (2001). Upper crustal structure in Puget Lowland, Washington: Results from the 1998 Seismic Hazards Investigation in Puget Sound. JGR Solid Earth 106(B7), 13541–13564. 10.1029/2001JB000154 — 3-D velocity to 11 km; Seattle, Tacoma, Hood Canal, SWIF; seismicity on Tacoma fault; basin contrasts bias 1-D locations.
2. Blakely, Wells, Weaver & Johnson (2002). Location, structure, and seismicity of the Seattle fault zone, Washington: Evidence from aeromagnetic anomalies, geologic mapping, and seismic-reflection data. GSA Bulletin 114(2), 169–177. 10.1130/0016-7606(2002)114<0169:LSASOT>2.0.CO;2 — Seattle fault geometry vs PNSN seismicity.
3. Preston, Creager, Crosson, Brocher & Tréhu (2003). Intraslab earthquakes: Dehydration of the Cascadia slab. Science 302(5648), 1197–1200. 10.1126/science.1090751 — Intraslab events split at 45 km Moho contour: updip slab mantle, downdip slab crust.
4. Bostock, Christensen & Peacock (2019). Seismicity in Cascadia. Lithos 332–333, 55–66. 10.1016/j.lithos.2019.02.019 — Margin-wide crustal/slab seismicity vs tremor band and fluid overpressure.
5. Bombardier, Cassidy & Dosso (2026). Does episodic tremor and slip influence seismicity in northern Cascadia? Seismica 5(1). 10.26443/seismica.v5i1.1953 — Six catalogs 2006–2024: fewer M>2.5 near ETS between episodes than GR predicts, slightly more during/after.
Alternates: Van Wagoner et al. 2002 JGR 107(B12) 10.1029/2001JB000710; Wells, Weaver & Blakely 1998 Geology 26 759 10.1130/0091-7613(1998)026<0759:FAMICA>2.3.CO;2; Sherrod et al. 2004 Geology 32 9 10.1130/G19914.1; Abers et al. 2009 Geology 37 1119–1122 10.1130/G30143A.1; Wech & Creager 2008 GRL 35 10.1029/2008GL035458; Angster et al. 2026 GSA Bull 138 3259–3278 10.1130/B38333.1.
Questions:
1. Is crustal microseismicity concentrated on Seattle/Tacoma/SWIF or diffuse? 3-D or DD relocation; distance to faults at depth vs randomized catalogs; mechanisms vs N–S compression. Limits: datum; q10 depth 1.1 km suggests blasts (remove PNSN explosions); basin bias; doubling partly from the new 122–123°W strip.
2. Does crustal/intraslab rate change with ETS (~5 episodes 2010–2016)? Stack around PNSN tremor episodes by depth class; vs Bombardier 2026. Limits: 5 episodes; effect at M>2.5 (few events, ML pending); tremor noise affects detection.
3. Do deep events split at the slab Moho (Preston 2003)? Sections vs McCrory slab and Preston Moho. Limits: few deep events; depth errors vs ~7 km crust; datum.
4. Completeness vs PNSN; do added events change b-value or depth distribution? Needs ML; ComCat anchoring across UW/PB/CC.
Not used: no peer-reviewed study of the 2014 M6.8 offshore Mendocino event found; Alongi 2021 not found; Crosson Puget papers not found; Dengler none (closest Oppenheimer et al. 1993 Science 261 433–438 10.1126/science.261.5120.433); no peer-reviewed Puget ML catalog; Rogers & Crosson 2002 GSC Open File; Shelly et al. 2024 Ferndale DOI unchecked.
