# Sources without a DOI (not in references.bib)

`references.bib` takes only entries resolved from a DOI by `utils/build_bib.py`. The sources below have no DOI.
They are cited in project notes (workflow/10_tomography/notes/science_framing.md on branch tomo/tiles). Citing one
in the paper needs a PI decision: a hand-written entry, or waiting for a published version with a DOI.
Metadata checked 2026-11-23 against the pages linked.
