Catalog v6.1 plan

Draft for the PI, 2026-11-26. Nothing in this plan runs before approval. v6.0 is registered as built (D-B100); v6.1 fixes what v6.0 deferred or got wrong.

In one paragraph. v6.1 keeps the v6.0 pipeline (NonLinLoc, source-specific station terms, waveform coherence) and changes four things: it uses the station-term recipe chosen today by a test that also scores published depths, it locates in model v0.4.2, it flags the Mount St Helens analog-signal events, and it applies the clock corrections and review flags that a person confirms. The machine cost is about 11 hours, so it fits one overnight run once the inputs are ready.

Items 1, 2 and 6 are computational and can start as soon as you approve. Items 3-5 need a person; two ways to handle them:

  • One version: wait for the reviews, then run v6.1 with everything (best if the reviews come within a few days).
  • Two versions: run v6.1 now with items 1, 2, 3 (flags only) and 6; put confirmed clock corrections and review flags into v6.2.

Acceptance targets for v6.1

  • 0 events above the ground or seafloor (hard requirement).
  • Axial tier A-B within 2 km of the seafloor at or above v5.1's 81 % (target 85 %, Waldhauser et al. 2020).
  • Median depth offsets against Hirao et al. (2025) and Lange et al. (2026) no worse than v5.1.
  • Held-out CC fit no worse than v6.0 beyond the 5 % margin of the depth test's rule.

Decisions you will be asked for

  1. Locate in v0.4.2 (recommended) or keep v0.4.1.
  2. One version or two (above).
  3. Accept the depth-test winner by the pre-registered rule, or look at the scores first.
