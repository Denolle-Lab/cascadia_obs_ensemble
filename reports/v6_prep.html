Draft for the PI, 2026-11-25. Catalog v6.0 is the NonLinLoc relocation of all 172,342 catalog v4.1 events in model v0.4.1, with the clock-corrected picks and the station-term method the PI chooses from the test in this report. It runs after the PI's review of the remaining clock-candidate epochs (D-B090). Station terms: what the test supports so far

Method test on the v5.1 grids (Axial and JdF complete on 2026-11-25 11:35; Blanco, Mount St Helens and the coherence arms finish in the background, see reloc_v6/ssst_test/run_g51_coh.log):

  • Axial (1,200 events, 16 station-phases): the v5.1 static terms give the best CC consistency without coherence (pair-demeaned CC residual MAD 12.3 ms; repeater pairs 0.134 km apart). The SSST schedule (9999, 16, 8, 4, 2 km) makes it worse step by step: 13.5 ms after the static step, 15.2 ms at the end, repeaters 0.242 km apart, floor-limited events 40 -> 111, A-B events within 2 km of the seafloor 91.4 % -> 85.7 %. Adding NLL-coherence on top of the final SSST gives 11.2 ms and 0.089 km, the best of all arms.
  • JdF (162 events, tier C): SSST frees the events from the floor (floor-limited 153 -> 23; median depth below the seafloor 0.02 -> 1.41 km). CC consistency is best at CharDist 16-8 km (35.7 ms against 39.0 ms static) and back to 40.9 ms at 2 km. Coherence does not apply (7 of 162 events have a compact pdf).

The options for the PI:

  • static (as v5.1): iter0, static terms, iter1.
  • regional SSST: Loc2ssst with large CharDist only (static step, then 16 and 8 km). Close to static at Axial, better at JdF; small CharDist (4, 2 km) is not supported by the test.
  • coherence: the step that sharpened Axial most, but it needs waveform CC for every family (today only the four test sets) and applies only to events with a compact pdf (ellipsoid len3 at most 5 km).

Native NLL-SSST at catalog scale

Loc2ssst (NonLinLoc 7.1.07) has fixed limits: 100,000 events per run (MAX_NUM_INPUT_FILES) and 20,000 arrivals per station-phase (MAX_NUM_ARRIVALS_LOC2SSST, more are dropped with an ERROR), and one output grid geometry (LSOUTGRID) for all station-phases of a run. The whole catalog has 2.5 million arrivals, so SSST runs per region:

  • blocks of tiles (25 km), each block's Loc2ssst reading the events of the block plus a halo of 3 x CharDist. Per tile the largest station-phase count is 15,334 arrivals (t07_32, Axial), below the limit; 13 station-phases exceed 20,000 over the whole catalog (B932.PB, B047.PB, AXEC1.OO, AXEC3.OO ...), so Axial blocks with halos must stay small;
  • LSOUTGRID = the block's NLLoc search box. The tiles hold 97,436 station-phases in total (median 41 per tile, max 603). With the production box (tile +- 75 km, 175 x 175 x 86 nodes, 10.5 MB per grid) one iteration writes about 1 TB; with tile +- 15 km (55 x 55 x 86, 1.0 MB) about 100 GB. The previous iteration is the next one's input, so two iterations are on disk at a time;
  • Loc2ssst cost: (LSGRID nodes) x (arrivals of the block) at about 26 ns each (measured: Axial 4.2e9 in 28.5 s and Blanco 5.7e9 in 36 s with 4 instances): with tile +- 15 km boxes and halos about 0.4 process-hours per iteration at LSGRID 4 km (CharDist 8-16 km), 3 at 2 km, 24 at 1 km;
  • station sets per Loc2ssst instance must not contain a station code that is a substring of another set's string (LSSTATIONS is matched with strstr); b4_v6_ssst.station_sets checks this.

An alternative that needs no new grids: apply each event's source-specific correction (the same Gaussian-weighted mean of its neighbours' residuals, computed at the event's previous position) to its pick times and rerun NLLoc with the static grids. It is the classic SSST of Richards-Dinger and Shearer (2000) rather than NonLinLoc's grid form; the correction does not follow the trial hypocentre inside one NLLoc search. Not built: the test does not support small CharDist, so it is not needed now.

Picks and clock corrections

The obs stage pins the pick file by md5 (PICKS, PICKS_MD5 in the chain). When the PI confirms more clock epochs (D-B090), the pick-timing toolkit writes a new pick file; the chain then reruns from obs (the grids do not depend on the picks). The pick file the catalog was located with goes into the catalog bundle (D-B080).

Location-review flags

ingest_review.py writes review_flag_not_earthquake.csv, review_depth_unreliable.csv and review_relocate.csv. b4_catalog.py joins them by ID when the catalog is built. They are flags only: no hypocentre is moved or removed (D-B055). A reviewer's corrected depth is kept as information, never written into depth.
