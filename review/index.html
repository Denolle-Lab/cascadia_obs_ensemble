Cascadia Catalog Reviews

Pages for human review of catalog v5.1 and of the pick timing, 25 November 2026. Answers are kept in your browser while you work; use each page's Download CSV button and send the file back.

v5.1 location problems

Four problems: deep events under Mount St Helens, the 2012 Brookings swarm, events with ComCat moment tensors, and deep-pass / far-only events. 1,512 rows; 342 events have full waveform pages.

  • Review viewer: summary plots, filterable table with record-section thumbnails, review fields, Load / Download CSV
  • Event reports: the four reports with one page per event (record sections, maps, residual tables)
  • review_v51_events.csv and README_review.md (columns, automatic rules, how to return)

Station clock candidates

Screen of every station for clock offsets, drifts and resets in the network-relative residuals. MH07.XD and MG07.XD are confirmed and corrected in the v6.0 picks; the 129 other candidate epochs are not applied until reviewed.

  • Clock screen report: residual time series and fitted models per station
  • review_candidates.csv: accept or reject each candidate epoch
