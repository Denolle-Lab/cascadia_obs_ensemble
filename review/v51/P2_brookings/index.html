Event views

How to read an event view. Three hypocentres are compared in the same v5.1 travel-time grids and station terms, so differences come from the location only: v5.1 (NonLinLoc maximum-likelihood point, run b4_v51_iter1 or its deep pass b4_v51_deep; solid lines, star), v4 (catalog v4 hypocentre; dashed, square), ComCat (matched ComCat hypocentre when one exists; dotted, diamond). Observed picks are black (the D-B072 re-timed picks NonLinLoc used). Predicted arrival = origin time + grid travel time + station term. Each hypothesis' origin time is refitted in the v5.1 grids (its own origin time + weighted mean of observed minus predicted, weights 1/(pick error² + 0.2²)), so the ticks and the 'OT refit' residuals compare locations, not origin times; residuals with the catalog origin times are given too. The v5.1 point is the NonLinLoc optimum for these picks in these grids (EDT likelihood, which down-weights inconsistent arrivals), so its rms is expected to be the smallest; a hypocentre with a similar rms fits the picks about as well in this model, and the residuals at the nearest stations and the NLLoc weights say more about depth than the overall rms. The travel time of each arrival comes from the grid NonLinLoc used for it in v5.1 (fine 1-km grid, or wide 2-km grid); the grid reader reproduces NonLinLoc's own predicted times at the v5.1 point to 0.001 s or better for every event (value in the header). Depth: km below sea level, true depth (Earth flattening undone).
Figures. (1) maps, E-W depth section with the NonLinLoc scatter samples, the D-B068 solid surface and Slab2 (Hayes et al. 2018) where it exists, pick times against distance; (2) record section on the v5.1 origin-time axis, vertical left, horizontals right, stations sorted by epicentral distance, short coloured ticks = predicted P (above the trace) and S (below), black = observed pick; stations without a pick (grey label) are the nearest ones with data; (3) zoom on P (vertical) and S (horizontal, or vertical when the station has none) at the nearest picked stations. Filters: zero-phase 2-corner Butterworth band-pass applied forward and backward (acausal: ringing can appear up to about 1/flow s before a sharp onset); band chosen per event by distance and magnitude (2-15 Hz if the median displayed distance is below 40 km, 1-10 Hz below 150 km, 1-6 Hz beyond, 1-5 Hz for M ≥ 4.5; the low corner stays at 1 Hz or above because the ocean microseism dominates below it) and stated on each figure. Each trace is scaled to its own peak in the window (both horizontals of a station share one scale): amplitudes cannot be compared between rows.
Waveforms. pnwstore short reads of the records around the window, on the MiniSEED record-header time base (the D-B072 definition), never a merged day; the picker's band rule (HH, else BH, else EH). NC and BK are not in pnwstore: they are read from NCEDC FDSN (the project's route for these networks, utils/data_client.py) on NCEDC's times. No instrument response is removed.
