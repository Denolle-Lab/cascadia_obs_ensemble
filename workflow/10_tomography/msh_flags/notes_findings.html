0. Findings and checks in short

  • The signal is wider than HSR, EDM and STD. The same pulse is injected at the same instant (to 0.1 s) on 25 UW analog channels: the 12 analog stations around the volcano, LCW, BOW, NLO, VG2, KMO, HBO and RNO (700-950 counts) and an inverted copy of about 3 counts on KOS, RVW, VLM, SSO, STW and RMW. No digital channel with data (CC, PB, TA) carries it. The rule uses all 25.
  • Timing. Two pulses every Wednesday, about 20 minutes apart, from 2011-05-18 to 2013-08-28, at 11:54-12:00 and 12:14-12:20 on the generator's clock, drifting 3.1 s earlier each week. The generator keeps the pre-2007 US daylight-saving rule, so on nine Wednesdays (March-early April, late October-early November) the pulses come at about 12:58 and 13:18 local time; a local-time window misses them. On four Wednesdays the plateau lasts from the first to the second pulse.
  • Result. 131 events certain (flagged), 5 probable, 3 mixed. 95 of the 131 are in the Mount St Helens box, and 82 of the 358 box events deeper than 30 km (v6.0) are flagged. Flagged events: v6.0 depth median 49 km (111 deeper than 30 km), every pick on signal channels for 127 of them (one late pick on a far station for the other 4), P picks on stations tens of km apart within 0.02-1.4 s of each other (no moveout). No flagged event has a ComCat match; their catalogue magnitudes are 0.9-3.2 (45 above 2.5), the size of the pulse, not of an earthquake.
  • Real earthquakes in the windows are not flagged. The three "mixed" events (27434, 63183, 66944) have 8-14 picks on stations without the signal, moveout of 3-7 s and no pick on a pulse edge (0 of 8 matched): real earthquakes that occurred within a minute or two of a pulse (example 27434 below). The guard keeps them out, and the waveform test agrees.
  • ComCat (ANSS 2010-2016, any event type, within 30 s and 150 km). None of the 131 certain and none of the 5 probable events is in ComCat, although 45 of them have catalogue magnitudes above 2.5 near a closely watched volcano. All three mixed events are there (PNSN earthquakes uw60408441, uw60555031, uw60576537, 11-16 km deep): the guard kept real earthquakes out. Of the 275 unflagged box events deeper than 30 km, 2 are in ComCat.
  • Waveform test on controls. 400 random events with picks on signal channels outside the windows: 0 pass (0 of 2,338 picks on a pulse edge). 80 box events on Wednesdays 11:00-13:30 device time outside the windows: 2 pass, both outside the windows so not flagged: 8194 (2010-08-04 13:23 PDT, five picks on the end of a pulse: an irregular calibration the scan did not cover) and 19900 (two picks of one station, JUN, on a step). In the windows, 949 of 1,022 picks sit on a pulse edge.
